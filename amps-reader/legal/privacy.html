Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 August 2026. Version tied to release 2026.08.05-03.

AMPS Library is published by Ananda Marga Pracaraka Samgha. Contact: dadaharii@gmail.com. Hosted policy: ampspublication.com/privacy (future: ampslibrary.org/privacy).

What this app stores on your device

  • Notes, bookmarks, highlights, journal, reading position, and settings in browser localStorage.
  • Downloaded content packs in IndexedDB metadata and Cache Storage file bodies.
  • Optional local backups you export as JSON files you choose to save or share.

What leaves the device

By default, reading data does not leave the device. Optional online behaviour may include:

  • Loading Google Fonts when online (browser may contact Google).
  • Optional Hindi summary translation via MyMemory if you use that helper.
  • Optional license/sync/TTS URLs that you configure yourself.

Not collected

No advertising identifiers. Analytics are disabled. Crash-reporting SDKs are not included. No AMPS cloud account is required for core offline reading.

Microphone

The first closed-beta / store slim-core profile excludes the shloka recorder and does not request microphone permission. Internal/full-offline builds may retain recording tools for institutional use; recordings stay on-device unless you export them.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics are disabled by default. Pack troubleshooting exports, if used, are local JSON downloads you control.

Your controls

See in-app Privacy & local data to export or delete notes, bookmarks, reading history, packs, and caches. Uninstalling the app removes app-managed storage on that device.
