Export and deletion of local data

Use Privacy & local data in the app for:

  • Export notes / bookmarks / full backup JSON
  • Clear notes, bookmarks, or reading history
  • Clear downloaded packs and obsolete caches
  • Reset all local app data (with confirmation)

Destructive actions ask for confirmation, explain what will be deleted, and can be cancelled. Operations work offline on app-managed storage only.
